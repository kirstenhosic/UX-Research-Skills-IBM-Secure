# Study at a glance

A one-page condensation of an existing research plan, for stakeholders who
will not read sixteen sections. **This is not a peer document to the plan.**
It is generated from a completed plan and updated when the plan changes;
if the two ever disagree, the plan is right. Do not use this as a
lightweight substitute for planning.

```
STUDY AT A GLANCE — [Study name]

Status:          Planning / Recruiting / In field / Analysis / Released
Product:         [named product]
Owner:           [researcher name]
Plan:            [link to the full research plan]
Last updated:    YYYY-MM-DD

THE DECISION
  [One sentence: what will change based on the findings, who decides,
   and by when.]

TOP RESEARCH QUESTIONS
  RQ1  [verbatim from the plan]
  RQ2  [verbatim from the plan]
  RQ3  [verbatim from the plan; list only the top 3]

METHOD & SAMPLE
  [Method], n=[target], [persona(s)], [participant type(s)]
  Biggest limitation: [one sentence on what this method cannot tell us]

TIMELINE
  Recruiting:    [dates]
  Sessions:      [dates]
  Readout:       [target date]

LINKS
  Tracker:       [participant tracker location — access restricted]
  Findings:      [added at release: report / deck links]
```

Keep it to one page. Anything that wants more room belongs in the plan, with
a link here.
