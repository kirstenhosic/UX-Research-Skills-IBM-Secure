# Study intake — is a study needed, and what is the lightest method that works?

Fill this out before writing a plan. Its job is to kill studies that should
not run and to right-size the ones that should. "No study" is a legitimate
and common outcome. The framework here is the Minimum Viable Research Method
(MVRM) from `select_best_method.md`: the lightest method that still produces
credible, actionable findings for the decision at hand.

```
STUDY INTAKE

Requested by:        [name, role]
Product / area:      [named product, not "IBM Secure"]
Date:                YYYY-MM-DD
Decision deadline:   [when the decision must be made]
```

## 1. The request

What was asked for, in the requester's words. Keep their framing; you will
challenge it below, not here.

## 2. The decision this informs

What will change based on the findings, who owns that decision, and by when.
**If no decision changes, stop here: no study.** Research that informs
nothing is waste regardless of how well it is run.

## 3. What we already know

Prior research, analytics, support tickets, sales and account feedback.
**If existing evidence already answers the question, stop here: no study.**
Cite the evidence in your response to the requester instead.

## 4. The real research question

Restate the request as a researchable question. Often the request arrives as
a solution ("test this design") when the real question is upstream ("do
operators understand this model at all?"). Write the question the decision
actually needs answered.

## 5. MVRM check

Evaluate candidate methods against these four criteria, in this order:

1. **Question fit.** Does the method actually answer the question? A method
   that does not has no viable minimum.
2. **Recruitment feasibility.** Can participants be recruited within the
   team's constraints in time for the decision? Confirm the current state of
   the standing constraints before leaning on them: no direct user access
   (customer recruitment routes through PMs and account teams and takes
   weeks), external SMEs as the faster alternative (directionally valid, not
   customer-specific, always disclosed as such), and senior technical
   participants (hard to recruit, low tolerance for loose studies).
3. **Minimum credible sample.** The smallest sample that produces defensible
   findings for this method and question. Benchmarks live in
   `select_best_method.md`; treat them as rules of thumb with stated
   assumptions, not guarantees.
4. **Decision stakes.** A low-stakes directional call with a two-week
   deadline warrants a different method than a strategic call with a
   three-month runway.

## 6. Recommendation

One of:

- **No study.** Say why (no decision, or evidence exists) and what to do
  instead.
- **Study.** Name the method, the target sample and persona, the rough
  timeline against the decision deadline, and the one biggest tradeoff you
  are accepting. State what the method cannot tell you; that limitation
  travels into the plan.

If the recommendation is a study, the next step is the
[research plan template](02-research-plan.md).
