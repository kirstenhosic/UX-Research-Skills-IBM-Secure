# Participant screener

The screener decides who gets into the study, which makes it the cheapest
place to protect the findings. A wrong participant costs a session; a wrong
screener costs the sample.

```
SCREENER — [Study name]

Target profile:   [persona, product, role — from plan §8]
Target n:         [count, per segment if segmented]
Quotas:           [e.g., at least 4 operators, at most 2 single-namespace]
Owner:            [who reviews responses and makes the call]
```

## Qualification criteria

Two explicit lists, written before the questions. Every question below must
serve one of these; delete any that serves neither.

- **Qualifies in:** [behaviors, responsibilities, and contexts that make
  someone the participant the study needs]
- **Qualifies out:** [disqualifiers: wrong role, no relevant recent
  experience, conflict of interest, participated in related study recently]

## Questions

Rules of craft:

1. **Screen on behavior, not identity.** "In the last 6 months, have you
   configured authentication for a team?" beats "Are you a platform
   engineer?" Titles vary; behavior is what the study needs.
2. **Never reveal the desired answer.** Multiple-choice options should make
   the qualifying answer indistinguishable from the others. If a candidate
   can guess what you want to hear, some will say it.
3. **Bound the recall window.** "In the last 6 months" rather than "ever."
4. **Collect it once.** Anything the screener captures does not get re-asked
   in the session (§4.6 item 19). Whatever the guide needs, hand it to the
   moderator via the tracker.
5. **For SME recruitment**, screen for role and task alignment with the
   actual user persona, not just industry. Findings from `sme-external`
   participants are disclosed as such, so the alignment has to be real.

```
Q1. [screening question]
    ( ) option — qualifies
    ( ) option — disqualifies
    ( ) option — disqualifies
```

## Handling responses

- Where responses are stored, who can see them, and the retention period
  (consistent with plan §14).
- What disqualified candidates are told. Thank them; do not tell them why
  they did not qualify, which teaches future candidates the answers.
- Qualified candidates go into the
  [participant tracker](06-participant-tracker.xlsx) with a P-ID assigned at
  entry. From this point on, the tracker is the only place identity and P-ID
  meet.
