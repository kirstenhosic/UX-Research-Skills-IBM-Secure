# IBM Secure UX Research — Design System & Output Standards

All research plans, deliverables, and documents generated by skills and agents in this repo follow a consistent design system and output standard.

## Purpose

Ensure that all artifacts produced by research skills maintain:
- **Professional quality** — Ready for stakeholder presentation
- **Consistent branding** — IBM HashiCorp Secure design language
- **Structural integrity** — Research findings are clear and traceable
- **Team alignment** — All outputs follow the same framework

## When to Apply

**Apply these standards to:**
- Research plans (via `research-plan-generator` skill)
- Readout decks (via `research-readout-deck/` generator)
- Data analysis outputs
- Competitive analysis matrices
- Any document generated for stakeholder review

**Skip these standards for:**
- Internal notes and scratch work
- De-identified transcripts (maintain anonymity over styling)
- Raw data files (CSVs, JSON, etc.)
- Working documents (use during analysis, discard after synthesis)

## Color Palette

All documents use IBM HashiCorp Secure's professional color scheme:

| Element | Color | RGB | Usage |
|---------|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary Title | Dark Blue | #1F4E79 | Research plan name, main headings |
| Section Heading | Light Blue | #2E75B6 | Section titles, subsection headings |
| Callout/Highlight | Light Gray | #D9E1F2 | Key findings, decision points, callout boxes |
| Body Text | Black | #000000 | All body text and paragraphs |
| Table Header | Light Gray | #D9E1F2 | Table headers and row separators |

**Rationale:** The blue color scheme echoes IBM and HashiCorp branding while remaining professional and accessible.

## Typography & Spacing

### Document Structure

**Title Page:**
- Main Title: 28pt, Bold, Primary Blue (#1F4E79)
- Subtitle: 18pt, Bold, Secondary Blue (#2E75B6)
- Metadata: 11pt, Regular Black
- Space Before Title: 152400 EMU (1 inch)
- Space After Title: 50800 EMU (0.35 inch)

**Headings:**
- Heading 1: 14pt, Bold, Primary Blue (#1F4E79)
- Space Before: 76200 EMU (0.5 inch)
- Space After: 63500 EMU (0.45 inch)

- Heading 2: 12pt, Bold, Secondary Blue (#2E75B6)
- Space Before: 50800 EMU (0.35 inch)
- Space After: 38100 EMU (0.25 inch)

**Body Text:**
- Regular: 11pt, Regular, Black
- Space Before Paragraph: 38100 EMU
- Space After Paragraph: 63500 EMU

**Lists:**
- Use "List Paragraph" style (not bullet points)
- 11pt, Regular
- Indent: 0.25"
- Space: 38100 EMU after each item

### Professional Spacing

- Section breaks: Generous white space (76200 EMU)
- Between paragraphs: Consistent 38100–63500 EMU
- After lists: 50800 EMU before next section
- Table spacing: 50800 EMU before and after

## Structural Elements

### Callout Boxes

Used for key information, decisions, and questions:
- Single-cell table with light gray background (#D9E1F2)
- Title in bold, Secondary Blue (#2E75B6)
- Content in regular black, 11pt
- Padding: 50800 EMU top and bottom
- Followed by 50800 EMU spacing

**When to use:**
- Central research question
- Key decision points
- Important constraints or assumptions
- Success criteria

### Tables

**Header Row:**
- Background: Light Gray (#D9E1F2)
- Text: Bold, Black, 11pt
- Every cell has a border (1pt, light gray)

**Data Rows:**
- Alternating row shading optional (improves readability on large tables)
- Text: Regular Black, 11pt
- Consistent column widths
- Clear cell borders

**Style:** "Light Grid Accent 1" (or equivalent)

## Research Plan Format

Every research plan generated follows this structure:

1. **Title & Metadata** (Strategic Framing)
   - Product name (Main Title, Primary Blue)
   - Plan type (Subtitle, Secondary Blue)
   - Context (metadata section)

2. **Purpose & Central Question**
   - Why this research matters
   - Central research question in callout box

3. **Scope Boundaries**
   - In-scope vs. out-of-scope comparison table
   - Clear constraints and rationale

4. **Core Research Questions**
   - Grouped by theme or priority
   - Mapped to decisions they inform

5. **Participants & Recruitment**
   - Target profile
   - Disqualifiers
   - Recruitment strategy with priority ordering

6. **Discussion Guide** (if included)
   - Sections with time allocations
   - Fully numbered questions (1.1, 2.1, 2.2, etc.)
   - Probes and moderator notes
   - Research question mappings

7. **Timeline & Milestones**
   - Week-by-week breakdown
   - Clear output/deliverable per phase

8. **Deliverables**
   - What the research will produce
   - Success criteria
   - Confidence notes

## Deck (Presentation) Format

Every readout deck follows this structure:

- **Slide Master:** IBM Carbon Design System or IBM Plex (corporate font)
- **Title Slides:** Bold heading, Professional blue
- **Content Slides:** Evidence → Interpretation → Insight → Recommendation
- **Visual Hierarchy:** Large headings (44pt), body text (24pt)
- **Callouts:** Key findings in color-highlighted boxes
- **Evidence Display:** Verbatim quotes with participant IDs
- **Recommendations:** Clear ownership and next steps

See `research-readout-deck/references/deck-structure.md` for full slide-by-slide guidance.

## Data Integrity Standards

Every output must include or reference:

### For Research Plans
- **Participant screener logic** — Exactly who you're talking to
- **Recruitment contingencies** — What if you don't hit your targets?
- **Analysis methodology** — How you'll reach findings
- **Scope boundaries** — Explicitly what's NOT being researched

### For Findings & Synthesis
- **Evidence traceability** — Every claim is linked to:
  - Verbatim quote + Participant ID (e.g., "P02 said: '...'")
  - Or: Observation in raw notes (e.g., "Session 3 notes, min 12:34")
- **Distinction between observation, interpretation, insight, recommendation**
  - Observation = What you heard/saw (not interpreted)
  - Interpretation = What it means in context
  - Insight = The pattern or tension (not a recommendation)
  - Recommendation = What to do about it (owned by a role)

### For Competitive Analysis
- **Claim labels:** [verified], [vendor claim], [inference], [unknown]
- **Source dating:** All volatile information dated
- **Visual evidence sourcing:** Every screenshot labeled by type ([live product], [marketing], [demo video], [third party])

## Quality Checklist

Before sharing any document, verify:

**Visual Design:**
- ✅ Title is Primary Blue (#1F4E79)
- ✅ Section headings are Secondary Blue (#2E75B6)
- ✅ Callout boxes have Light Gray (#D9E1F2) background
- ✅ Spacing is consistent (no cramped text)
- ✅ Tables have colored headers

**Structural Integrity:**
- ✅ Research plan has scope boundaries (in/out of scope)
- ✅ All claims reference evidence (quotes + participant IDs)
- ✅ Findings distinguish observation → interpretation → insight
- ✅ Recommendations have named owners
- ✅ Deliverables are clear and achievable

**Professional Quality:**
- ✅ No orphaned headings
- ✅ No half-empty tables
- ✅ Consistent formatting throughout
- ✅ Proofread and spell-checked
- ✅ Ready for stakeholder presentation
