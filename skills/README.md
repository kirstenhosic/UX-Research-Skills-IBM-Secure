# IBM HashiCorp Secure Research Plan Generator

Create professional, reusable research plan documents following IBM HashiCorp Secure's design system and formatting standards.

## What This Skill Does

Generates polished Word documents (.docx) for UX research plans with:
- **Consistent Design System**: Blue color scheme (#1F4E79, #2E75B6), professional typography
- **Flexible Structure**: Adapts to different research types (generative, evaluative, comparative)
- **Callout Boxes**: Highlights key requirements, questions, and decisions
- **Professional Layouts**: Proper spacing, heading hierarchy, table formatting
- **Team Reusability**: Use for Vault, Boundary, Consul, or other HashiCorp Secure products

## How to Use

When you invoke this skill, you'll provide:

1. **Research Plan Type** (choose one):
   - Migration Study (like Consul)
   - Feature Evaluation
   - Adoption & Usability
   - Competitive Analysis
   - Custom

2. **Core Details**:
   - Product name
   - Team/stakeholders
   - Key research questions (up to 9)
   - Target participants
   - Timeline

3. **Optional Customizations**:
   - Include/exclude specific sections
   - Add custom hypotheses
   - Adjust participant recruitment strategy
   - Modify analysis methodology

## Output

A Word document (.docx) ready to share with stakeholders, print, or refine. Includes:

- Professional cover with metadata
- Strategic framing and scope
- Research questions mapped to decisions
- Participant profile and recruiting strategy
- Detailed discussion/study guide (fully numbered for field reference)
- Timeline and milestones
- Deliverables and success criteria
- Optional: ethics, consent, analysis methodology

## Design System

All documents use IBM HashiCorp Secure's brand-aligned styling:
- **Primary Blue** (#1F4E79) for main titles
- **Secondary Blue** (#2E75B6) for section headings
- **Light Gray** (#D9E1F2) for callout boxes
- **Strategic Spacing** (38100–152400 EMU) for readability
- **List Paragraph Style** for professional bullet points

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Migration Research (like Consul)
Use this template to research how customers make migration decisions, what barriers exist, and what support they need.

### Scenario 2: Feature Evaluation
Test a new feature or product concept with target users. Generate a research plan that maps to feature-specific questions and success metrics.

### Scenario 3: Adoption Study
Research why teams do or don't adopt your product, what triggers adoption, and what removes friction.

### Scenario 4: Multi-Product Comparison
Plan research comparing multiple products or versions. Includes comparative questions and preference mapping.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Be Specific**: The more detail you provide about your research context, the better tailored the document
- **Know Your Research Questions**: Even rough draft RQs help generate targeted guidance
- **Participant Clarity**: Clear participant profiles lead to better screener logic
- **Timeline Reality**: Provide realistic timelines; the skill will suggest phase breakdowns
- **Scope Boundaries**: Explicitly note what's OUT of scope so stakeholders understand constraints
